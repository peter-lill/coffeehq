# GS-004 — Section Selection Rules

Version: 1.0

The interaction determines the structure. Do not include a heading merely because it exists.

## Available sections

Lodgement; Employment; Injury; Medical; Rehabilitation & RTW; Early Psychological Support; Employer Information; Witness Information; Liability; Discussion; Outcome; Action.

## Selection rules

- Lodgement: include only for material application, authority, certificate or claim-registration matters.
- Employment: include only for new or clarified employment information.
- Injury: include for new or clarified injury circumstances, causative factors, allegations, symptoms or relevant dates.
- Medical: include for diagnosis, first presentation, certification, treatment, referral, medication or a material medical change.
- Rehabilitation & RTW: include only when capacity, suitable duties, rehabilitation or return to work is discussed.
- Early Psychological Support: include where early treatment support for a psychological injury is explained or arranged.
- Employer Information: include where the employer provides evidence, process information, a response or agreed action.
- Witness Information: include where a witness is identified, contacted or provides evidence.
- Liability: include where causation, reasonable management action, procedural fairness or another liability consideration is materially discussed.
- Discussion: use for material claim-management advice or clarification not better placed elsewhere.
- Outcome: include where the interaction changes the claim position or confirms the immediate result.
- Action: include whenever future work remains.

## Order

Lodgement → Employment → Injury → Medical → Rehabilitation & RTW → Early Psychological Support → Employer/Witness Information → Liability → Discussion → Outcome → Action.

## Omission rules

- Do not create an Administration heading.
- Do not duplicate a fact across sections.
- Remove any section that contains only trivial or routine information.
- A voicemail or unsuccessful contact generally requires only Discussion, Outcome and Action.
- A short administrative contact may require only Outcome and Action.
