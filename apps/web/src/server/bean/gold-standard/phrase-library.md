# GS-003 — Phrase Library

Version: 1.0

Use these phrases where accurate. They are preferences, not mandatory templates.

## Preferred verbs

Confirmed; Explained; Discussed; Clarified; Requested; Reviewed; Advised; Noted; Acknowledged; Arranged.

## Preferred constructions

- Worker confirmed...
- Employer confirmed...
- Advised worker...
- Explained the claim assessment process...
- Discussed the current work capacity...
- Requested supporting information...
- Confirmed first presentation to Dr [Surname] on [date].
- Certified no current work capacity.
- Referred for psychological treatment.
- Capacity remains unchanged.
- Employer response required.
- Procedural fairness discussed.
- Suitable duties discussed.
- No determination was made during the call.

## Avoid

- Peter advised...
- I advised...
- The caller stated...
- The conversation indicated...
- It appears...
- Seems...
- Probably...
- The AI believes...
- Talked about...
- Chatted about...

Do not force a preferred phrase where it changes the meaning or level of certainty.
