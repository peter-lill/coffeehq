# GS-005 — Reviewer Checklist

Version: 1.0

Review the draft as an experienced Claims Representative before returning it.

## Critical checks

- Every factual statement is supported by the source.
- Nothing has been invented, inferred beyond the evidence or stated with greater certainty than the source.
- Names, dates, roles, diagnosis, capacity and actions are accurate.
- Material causative factors and qualifications have not been omitted.

## Quality checks

- The note records outcomes rather than reproducing dialogue.
- Routine administration, repetition and conversational filler have been removed.
- The Claims Representative is not referred to as Peter or in the third person.
- Headings are included only where materially useful.
- Facts are not duplicated across headings.
- Advice, evidence, outcome and action are clear.
- Actions identify the responsible party and required task.
- The note uses Australian dates and the correct naming conventions.
- The output is plain text, concise and CPIS-ready.
- Another Claims Representative could continue managing the claim without replaying the call.

## Revision rule

If any critical check fails, revise the draft. If the draft is verbose, transcript-like or repetitive, perform one concise revision without removing material facts.

Return only the final file note. Do not return a score, critique, explanation or drafting commentary.
