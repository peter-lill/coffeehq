# Interaction Profile — Worker Initial Contact (IBC)

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Produce a concise initial-contact file note that captures the information needed to progress claim assessment without reproducing the intake script.

## Opening

Use:

`IBC – [Worker full name] – Initial claim discussion`

Add a brief injury type or purpose only where supported and useful.

## Usually material

- application or authority issues affecting validity;
- employer, employment status, position, commencement and secondary employment where newly confirmed;
- date of injury or first onset;
- causative factors identified by the worker;
- first medical presentation, diagnosis, certification, treatment and referrals;
- current work capacity and return-to-work position;
- early psychological treatment information for psychological injuries;
- material advice about assessment, evidence required and next steps;
- clear outcomes and actions.

## Usually omit

- identity-verification questions;
- call-recording notice;
- greetings and rapport;
- payroll or banking identifiers;
- routine explanation of every benefit or provider type;
- repeated Worker Assist instructions;
- questions asked where the answer can be recorded directly;
- information already established that did not change.

## Structure

Select only sections supported by material content. Common sections are Lodgement, Employment, Injury, Medical, Rehabilitation & RTW, Early Psychological Support, Discussion, Outcome and Action.

## Quality target

The note must allow the next Claims Representative to identify the alleged injury circumstances, medical position, current capacity, investigation direction and immediate outstanding actions. It should normally be materially shorter than the transcript and should not read like an investigation summary.
